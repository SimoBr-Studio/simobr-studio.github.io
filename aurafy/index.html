The idea

Questions you already ask yourself

Who in my circle actually loves me? Is this person a red flag? Why do I keep my walls up? Why people keep it

Built differently, on purpose

Fully offline

Every reading is computed on your device. Airplane mode? Still works. No servers exist.

Radically private

No account, no sign-up, no cloud. Names you type and answers you give never leave your phone.

Four languages, really

English, French, Spanish — and full right-to-left Arabic. Authored by hand, not machine-translated.

Stars ✦ — never money

A free in-app currency you earn by showing up. It unlocks readings and themes. There is nothing to buy.

Share cards

Every result becomes a beautiful image card — save it, post it, or keep it to yourself.

Themes & streaks

Unlockable visual themes, daily quotes, and a streak that rewards coming back.

Made for reflection and entertainment. Aurafy is not a psychological assessment, diagnosis, therapy, or professional advice of any kind — it's a mirror, held with care.
Privacy in one paragraph

No servers. No accounts. No data trail.

Aurafy has nothing to collect your data with. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, which serves the ads that keep the app free. The full detail lives in the Privacy Policy.
